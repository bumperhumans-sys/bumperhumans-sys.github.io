The Game

What Is Bubble Soccer?

Picture soccer — except every player is strapped inside a giant inflatable bubble from the knees up. Bumping, bouncing, and full-speed collisions aren't fouls; they're the whole point.

The padded suits absorb every impact, so players of any age or skill level can crash into each other, roll across the field, and pop right back up unhurt. No experience needed — if you can run (or wobble), you can play.

Games run in short, high-energy rounds so everyone gets plenty of field time — and plenty of time to catch their breath between fits of laughter.

Where We Play

Serving the Peninsula

Based in Santa Clara, CA, we offer free delivery within a 25-mile radius and serve events across the greater South Bay and Peninsula. Outside our service radius? No problem — a delivery charge applies based on distance.

We play at parks, gyms, backyards, and corporate campuses — anywhere with a flat grass or turf area roughly the size of a basketball court.

Venue reservations and any required park permits are the responsibility of the renter. Happy to suggest parks that have worked well for past events.
